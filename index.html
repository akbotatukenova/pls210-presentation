EPISTEMOLOGICAL POSITIONING

Why interpretivism?

I am not asking

“What percentage of users think this is cruel?”

I am asking

“What do harm, cruelty, sport, and tradition mean to the people discussing them?”

  • Meanings are contextual and may be contested.
  • I do not fix participants’ meanings in advance.
  • The researcher also interprets meaning, so reflexivity matters.
Studying interpretations of harm does not mean physical suffering is unreal. Veterinary determination is simply not the question this design answers.

Schwartz-Shea & Yanow (2012); Farrow et al. (2020)

RETURN TO THE OPENING QUESTION

The line is itself the object of study.

The question is not only where I draw the line between sport, tradition, harm, and cruelty.

It is how people debating the same controversy draw that line for themselves — and what they invoke to justify it.

That boundary-making is what this research seeks to understand.
